Polytope Tropes

🎮 Click Image to Start Game in Browser...

This is an example game developed in Godot Engine and exported to HTML, where I'm figuring-out general platformer-like gaming functionality. You control a shape-shifting polytope moving around a scene and interacting with various surfaces and other polytopes.

There's every chance that this is broken and you may have to manually refresh the page if you fall off the screen. I'm trying to build it into something functional, as like a demo of various aspects of Godot as I teach myself how to develop in it.

If you wanna see the source code, it's over here on GitHub: learning-gamedev-godot4 and should somewhat regularly aligned to the version here on this site, but you can always confirm by aligning the version shown here with the polytope-tropes-v_._._ Git Tag on the repo.
