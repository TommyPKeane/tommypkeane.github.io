You're here, I'm me, and what's all this?

Hiya! I'm Tommy P. Keane -- not any one of the famous ones -- and this is my professional-ish personal infotainment website all about me and my career and creative endeavours.

All y'all may be wondering: "Tommy, who are you, though?" and then you may immediately wanna frantically qualify that with "-- in 100 words or less! Preferably less! Less is more! Please! We aren't readin' all of that, but we're happy (or sad?) for you, or whatever."

So yeah, I'm vegan, I was born in the mid 1980s, I'm a reasonably debt-laden home owner in the suburban marshlands of Western Massachussetts in the Connecticut River Valley, and I'm in a functionally persistent state of agoraphobic anxiety.

Professionally, I am (and have been) a cashier, a shopping cart wrangler, an IT/Networking Technician, a Graduate Research Assistant, a Teaching Assistant, a Guest Lecturer, a Research Lab Assistant, an Embedded Systems Software Engineer for SATCOM Systems, a Data Scientist, a DevOps Engineer, a Full-Stack Web Developer, a Cloud Systems Architect, a Computer-Vision Machine-Learning Research, an MLOps Engineer.

Whoa ... I think I actually said all that in under 100 words. That might be a first!
